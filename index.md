# ✋ Hi!

I am Nicolas, a third-year Economics PhD student at the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU Vienna). I am interested in policy transmission dynamics that involve networks or micro-macro interactions. 

# 📝 Research projects
- Mapping the International Transmission of Macroeconomic Shocks: A GVAR Analysis with multiple networks, with Gabriel Konecny (Work in Progress)
- Stock market spillovers of US monetary policy: Policy transmission in spatial autoregressive models with multiple networks, with Gabriel Konecny (Work in Progress)
- Accounting rules: Exact Restrictions in Multivariate Time-Series Models (Work in Progress)
